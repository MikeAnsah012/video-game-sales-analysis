# Gaming Industry Sales Performance Analysis

# Executive Summary
This project analyzes sales performance in the gaming industry to identify the key drivers of revenue across genres, platforms, and regions. The analysis shows that sales are largely driven by a few high-performing genres and platforms, with North America contributing the majority of revenue.
Findings also indicate that revenue is concentrated among a small number of top-performing games, while most titles generate relatively low sales. Additionally, higher-quality games, reflected by stronger critic scores, tend to perform better.
Overall, the analysis highlights opportunities to expand into underperforming regions, focus on high-demand genres, and reduce reliance on a small number of top-performing titles.

# Business Problem
The gaming company lacks clear visibility into what is driving sales performance across different genres, platforms, and regions. Revenue is generated across thousands of titles, but it is unclear which factors contribute most to success and where growth opportunities exist. Additionally, there is a risk of over-reliance on a small number of high-performing games and specific regions.
